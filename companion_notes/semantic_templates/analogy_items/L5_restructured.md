**What this level tests.** The clause said again with another syntax (nominalisation, passive, possessive). The foil keeps the frame and the words of the clause and replaces the event ("approval" by "attention").

**What to check:**
- Is each restructured sentence the same statement as the clause?
- Is each foil a different event, not implied by the clause?
- Are the denials natural?
